data/borders.npz -- country outlines used to keep each national elevation survey inside its own country.

Derived from geo-maps "countries-maritime-1m" v0.6.0 (https://github.com/simonepri/geo-maps), which is extracted from
OpenStreetMap. Built with tools/build_borders.py (only the countries the app clips, split into 1-degree pieces).
